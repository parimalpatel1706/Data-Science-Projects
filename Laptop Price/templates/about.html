About This App

What is Laptop Price Predictor?

This web application uses machine learning to predict the price of a laptop based on its specifications such as brand, processor, RAM, storage, and other technical attributes. It is designed for students, tech enthusiasts, resellers, and buyers who want to estimate the fair value of a device.

The prediction is based on real-world data and trained models, making it a practical and fun tool to experiment with laptop configurations.
